BALTIC WATER TEMPERATURE

How warm is the Baltic today?

Sea-water temperature for 53 cities on the Baltic coast, averaged live from up to seven independent meteorological and satellite sources — plus a 30-day history, a 10-day forecast, and the nearest beach to swim at.

Free, no ads No account needed Works in 9 languages

What it does

🌊

53 cities, one map

Every major city and coastal town on the Baltic, from Copenhagen to Saint Petersburg, plotted on a live map with the current water temperature.

📡

Seven sources, averaged

Satellite analyses, ocean models, and coastal stations are combined into one honest number — the app shows exactly how many sources agree.

📈

30-day history

See how the water has warmed or cooled over the past month before deciding whether today is the day.

🔮

10-day forecast

An ocean-model prediction of where the temperature is headed, not just where it's been.

🏖️

Nearby beaches

Curated beaches near each city, with photos, so you know exactly where to go once the water's warm enough.

🌐

Nine languages

Fully translated interface, no auto-translate guesswork — English, Russian, German, Polish, Lithuanian, Latvian, Finnish, Swedish, and Dutch.

Where the numbers come from

Every reading is fetched live and averaged only from whichever sources currently have data — nothing is estimated or invented.

  • Open‑Meteo MarineOcean model based on Copernicus Marine / ECMWF data, plus the 10-day forecast
  • NASA JPL MUR SSTMulti-satellite sea-surface-temperature analysis, 1 km grid
  • NOAA OISST v2.1Optimum interpolation from satellites, ships and buoys, 25 km
  • NOAA Geo‑Polar BlendedCoastWatch blended geostationary + polar satellite analysis, 5 km
  • SMHISwedish Meteorological and Hydrological Institute, coastal observations
  • FMIFinnish Meteorological Institute, mareograph stations
  • IMGW‑PIBPolish Institute of Meteorology, coastal hydrological gauges
